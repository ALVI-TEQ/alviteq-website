ALVITEQ Corporate Identity — Approved 03D Production Assets

Official colours
Blue: #0D47FF
Teal: #00B2A9
Navy: #0B1D3A
Reversed: #FFFFFF

Master-artwork rules
- Flat colours only.
- No gradients.
- No transparency blending.
- No shadows or blur.
- No overlap/purple junction colour.
- Keep clear space of at least one icon stroke width.
- Minimum digital icon size: 16 px.

Files
- alviteq-icon.svg — primary full-colour master icon
- alviteq-icon-white.svg — reversed master
- alviteq-icon-navy.svg — single-colour navy
- alviteq-icon-black.svg — single-colour black
- alviteq-horizontal.svg — horizontal icon + wordmark lockup
- PNG icon sizes: 16 through 1024 px
- favicon.ico — 16/32/48 px
- alviteq-horizontal-1500.png — transparent horizontal lockup

Note
The icon has been reconstructed as clean vector geometry from the approved 03D visual direction.
Before trademark filing or large-format manufacturing, a professional vector designer should do a final
Bezier/optical review and convert the wordmark typography to outlines.
